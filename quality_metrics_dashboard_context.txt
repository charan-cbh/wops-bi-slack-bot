QUALITY METRICS DASHBOARD CONTEXT FOR BI QUERY GENERATION

=== DASHBOARD: Quality Assurance (QA) Score Analytics ===

DASHBOARD PURPOSE:
Track organizational-level quality performance through Klaus QA reviews and scoring to monitor service quality standards and identify improvement opportunities for the Worker Operations department.

USED BY:
- Senior Leadership: Quality performance oversight and strategic quality management
- Operations Directors: Department-wide quality monitoring and improvement initiatives
- QA Management: Organizational quality trends and benchmark tracking
- Analytics Team: Quality metrics reporting for executive dashboards

KEY BUSINESS QUESTIONS:
- "What's our overall QA score this week?"
- "Show me weekly QA score trends for the organization"
- "How many QA audits were completed?"
- "What's our organizational quality performance?"

=== PRIMARY TABLES ===
1. DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_KLAUS__REVIEWS - Klaus QA review data
2. DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_ZENDESK__MQR_TICKETS - Ticket data for joining

KEY QA METRICS:
- Overall_Score: Calculated QA score (0-100 scale)
- Total_Audits: Number of QA reviews completed
- Average_QA_Score: Mean quality score across all audits
- Pass_Fail_Status: Pass/Fail determination based on scorecard thresholds

SCORECARD FILTERING:
Valid Scorecard IDs: 53921, 54262, 59144, 60047, 56287, 60363
Primary Scorecards for reporting: 53921, 60047

QA SCORE THRESHOLDS:
- Scorecards 53921, 54262, 60047: Pass >= 85%
- Scorecard 59144: Pass >= 90%
- Target Score: 85%+ for most scorecards

QA SCORE QUERY PATTERN:
BUSINESS QUESTIONS: "What's our weekly QA score?", "Show me QA trends", "How many audits this week?"

SQL TEMPLATE:
SELECT 
    DATE_TRUNC('week', tickets.SOLVED_AT_PST) AS week_start,
    AVG(qa_data.Overall_Score) AS average_qa_score,
    COUNT(*) AS total_audits
FROM (
    -- Klaus QA Data Processing CTE
    WITH KlausBase AS (
        SELECT
            klaus.REVIEW_ID,
            REGEXP_REPLACE(TICKET_URL, '.*/tickets/', '') as TICKET_ID,
            SCORECARD_ID,
            SCORECARD_NAME,
            RATING_CATEGORY_ID,
            RATING_SCORE,
            RATING_WEIGHT,
            REVIEWEE_EMAIL,
            REVIEWEE_NAME,
            REVIEW_CREATED_AT
        FROM DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_KLAUS__REVIEWS as klaus
        WHERE SCORECARD_ID IN (53921, 54262, 59144, 60047, 56287, 60363)
    ),
    PivotedRatings AS (
        SELECT
            REVIEW_ID,
            CASE WHEN SUM(RATING_WEIGHT) = 0 THEN NULL
                 ELSE SUM(RATING_SCORE * RATING_WEIGHT) / SUM(100 * RATING_WEIGHT) * 100
            END as Overall_Score
        FROM KlausBase
        GROUP BY REVIEW_ID
    )
    SELECT 
        kb.TICKET_ID,
        kb.SCORECARD_ID,
        pr.Overall_Score
    FROM KlausBase kb
    JOIN PivotedRatings pr ON kb.REVIEW_ID = pr.REVIEW_ID
    WHERE kb.REVIEWEE_EMAIL IS NOT NULL
) qa_data
LEFT JOIN DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_ZENDESK__MQR_TICKETS tickets 
    ON qa_data.TICKET_ID = tickets.TICKET_ID
WHERE qa_data.SCORECARD_ID IN (53921, 60047)
    AND tickets.SOLVED_AT_PST >= {START_DATE}
    AND tickets.SOLVED_AT_PST < {END_DATE}
    AND tickets.STATUS IN ('closed', 'solved')
GROUP BY DATE_TRUNC('week', tickets.SOLVED_AT_PST)
ORDER BY week_start ASC

=== DASHBOARD: Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) Analytics ===

DASHBOARD PURPOSE:
Monitor organizational customer satisfaction levels through post-resolution surveys to track service quality from the customer perspective across the Worker Operations department.

USED BY:
- Senior Leadership: Customer satisfaction oversight and strategic customer experience management
- Operations Directors: Department-wide customer satisfaction monitoring
- Customer Experience Team: Satisfaction trends and improvement tracking
- Analytics Team: CSAT metrics for executive reporting

KEY BUSINESS QUESTIONS:
- "What's our CSAT score this week?"
- "Show me weekly CSAT trends"
- "How many CSAT surveys were completed?"
- "What's our customer satisfaction performance?"

PRIMARY TABLE: DBT_PRODUCTION.RPT_CSAT_SURVEY_DETAILS

KEY CSAT METRICS:
- CSAT_SCORE_BINARY: Binary satisfaction score (0 or 1)
- Average CSAT: Mean customer satisfaction percentage
- Survey Count: Total number of completed surveys
- SOLVED_AT_PST: Ticket resolution date for trending

CSAT PERFORMANCE BENCHMARKS:
- Excellent: 95%+ satisfaction
- Good: 90%+ satisfaction  
- Target: 85%+ satisfaction
- Needs Improvement: <85% satisfaction

CSAT QUERY PATTERN:
BUSINESS QUESTIONS: "What's our CSAT this week?", "Show me customer satisfaction trends", "Weekly CSAT performance"

SQL TEMPLATE:
SELECT 
    DATE_TRUNC('week', SOLVED_AT_PST) AS week_start,
    AVG(CSAT_SCORE_BINARY) AS average_csat_score,
    COUNT(*) AS total_surveys
FROM DBT_PRODUCTION.RPT_CSAT_SURVEY_DETAILS
WHERE SOLVED_AT_PST >= {START_DATE}
    AND SOLVED_AT_PST < {END_DATE}
GROUP BY DATE_TRUNC('week', SOLVED_AT_PST)
ORDER BY week_start ASC

=== DASHBOARD: First Contact Resolution (FCR) Analytics ===

DASHBOARD PURPOSE:
Track organizational ability to resolve customer issues on the first contact, measuring efficiency and customer experience quality across Chat and Voice channels for the Worker Operations department.

USED BY:
- Senior Leadership: Operational efficiency oversight and strategic process improvement
- Operations Directors: Department-wide resolution efficiency monitoring
- Process Improvement Team: FCR trends and optimization opportunities
- Analytics Team: Efficiency metrics for executive dashboards

KEY BUSINESS QUESTIONS:
- "What's our FCR rate this week?"
- "Show me first contact resolution trends"
- "How much repeat contact volume do we have?"
- "FCR performance for Chat and Voice combined"

PRIMARY TABLE: DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_ZENDESK__MQR_TICKETS (with complex FCR logic)

KEY FCR METRICS:
- FCR Rate: Percentage of issues resolved on first contact
- Repeat Contact Volume: Number of follow-up contacts within 24 hours
- IS_RESOLVED_FIRST_TIME: Binary indicator (1 = resolved first time, 0 = repeat contact)
- Contact Channels: Chat and Voice combined for FCR calculation

FCR CALCULATION LOGIC:
A ticket is considered "resolved first time" if:
1. No follow-up ticket from same requester within 24 hours
2. Only applies to Chat and Voice channels
3. Must be from same requester (REQUESTER_ID)

FCR PERFORMANCE BENCHMARKS:
- Excellent: 90%+ FCR rate
- Good: 85%+ FCR rate
- Target: 80%+ FCR rate  
- Needs Improvement: <80% FCR rate

FCR QUERY PATTERN:
BUSINESS QUESTIONS: "What's our FCR rate?", "Show me FCR trends", "Repeat contact volume"

SQL TEMPLATE:
SELECT 
    DATE_TRUNC('week', CREATED_AT_PST) AS week_start,
    (CAST(SUM(CASE WHEN IS_RESOLVED_FIRST_TIME = 1 
                    AND CONTACT_CHANNEL IN ('Chat', 'Voice') THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS DOUBLE) /
     NULLIF(CAST(SUM(CASE WHEN CONTACT_CHANNEL IN ('Chat', 'Voice') THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS DOUBLE), 0)) * 100 AS fcr_rate,
    SUM(CASE WHEN IS_RESOLVED_FIRST_TIME = 0 
             AND CONTACT_CHANNEL IN ('Chat', 'Voice') THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS repeat_contact_volume
FROM (
    WITH FCR_CTE AS (
        SELECT
            ticket_id,
            requester_id,
            CASE WHEN group_id = '17837476387479' THEN 'Chat'
                 WHEN group_id = '28949203098007' THEN 'Voice'
                 ELSE 'Other' END AS contact_channel,
            created_at_pst,
            CASE WHEN created_at_pst + INTERVAL '24 HOUR' >= 
                      LEAD(created_at_pst) OVER (PARTITION BY requester_id ORDER BY created_at_pst)
                 THEN 0 ELSE 1 END AS is_resolved_first_time
        FROM DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_ZENDESK__MQR_TICKETS
        WHERE STATUS IN ('solved', 'closed')
            AND brand_id IN ('360002340693', '29186504989207')
            AND channel IN ('native_messaging')
            AND group_id IN ('17837476387479', '28949203098007')
            AND ASSIGNEE_ID != '15840273336343'
            AND TICKET_TAGS NOT LIKE '%email_blocked%'
            AND TICKET_TAGS NOT LIKE '%bulk_email_tool%'
            AND TICKET_TAGS NOT LIKE '%seattle_psst_monthly_email%'
            AND created_at_pst >= {DATE_RANGE}
    )
    SELECT * FROM FCR_CTE
) fcr_data
WHERE CREATED_AT_PST >= {START_DATE}
    AND CREATED_AT_PST < {END_DATE}
GROUP BY DATE_TRUNC('week', CREATED_AT_PST)
ORDER BY week_start ASC
