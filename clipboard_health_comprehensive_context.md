## Platform Overview

**What is Clipboard Health?**
Clipboard Health is a comprehensive healthcare staffing platform that connects healthcare facilities with licensed healthcare professionals (CNAs, LVNs, RNs, and other specialties) for shift-based work. The platform operates through both mobile applications and a web portal, providing real-time scheduling, billing, and workforce management solutions.

**Core Business Model:**
- Facilities post available shifts on the platform marketplace
- Healthcare professionals browse and book available shifts
- Clipboard Health charges facilities hourly rates for booked shifts
- All shifts are eligible for instant payment to workers after timesheet submission
- Platform operates 24/7 with automated systems and human support

**Key Value Propositions:**
- **For Facilities**: Instant access to qualified healthcare professionals, reduced administrative burden, flexible staffing solutions
- **For Professionals**: Flexible work schedules, instant payment options, access to multiple facilities, professional development opportunities

## Shift Management System

### Shift Posting Process
**Best Practices for Shift Posting:**
- Post shifts as soon as they become available (not closer to scheduled dates)
- Earlier posting significantly increases booking likelihood
- Shifts can be posted from mobile app or desktop web portal
- Use competitive rates to ensure shifts attract qualified professionals

**Priority Access System:**
- **Favorites List**: Professionals on facility's favorites list get priority booking window
- **Priority Window**: Opens when shifts are first posted, before general marketplace access
- **InstantBook Feature**: Majority of facilities have this enabled for immediate shift confirmation

**Shift Invites Feature:**
- Facilities can invite specific professionals to work particular shifts
- Invited shifts are visible only to the invited professional initially
- Option to open to favorites list if invitation is declined
- Custom rates can be set for invited professionals

### Urgent Shifts System
**Automatic Triggering Conditions:**
- Professional cancels within 0-8 hours before shift start time
- Professional cancels within 0-2 hours after shift start time
- Shifts removed from professional calendars on short notice
- Anticipated no-shows based on GPS tracking

**Urgent Shift Benefits:**
- Posted at premium rates to attract immediate coverage
- No additional cost to facilities
- Automated system reduces administrative burden
- Higher likelihood of last-minute coverage

**Manual Urgent Shift Requests:**
- Facilities can manually request urgent shifts for immediate needs
- Available through both mobile app and web portal
- Support team assistance available for complex situations

### Skills Assessment Requirements
**CNA Skills Assessment:**
- Video-based evaluation testing key nursing assistant skills
- Required for newer professionals to book shifts at facilities
- Ensures competency standards are met before shift assignment
- Conducted remotely through the Clipboard Health platform

## Cancellation Policy and Fees

### Facility Cancellation Rules
**Cancellation Fee Structure:**
- Cancellations within contract-specified timeframe incur fees at agreed-upon rates
- Cancellations outside contract timeframe have no cancellation fees
- Fees are calculated based on individual facility contract terms

**Late Arrival Exceptions:**
- Cancellation fees waived for professionals with 30+ minute late ETAs
- Facilities can cancel late professionals at no charge
- Contact Clipboard Health support for immediate assistance with late arrivals

### Professional No-Show Procedures
**No-Show Processing:**
- Can be processed via web or mobile app when shift begins
- Available as soon as professional enters facility geofence
- Option to request replacement professional at no additional charge
- Automatic systems monitor professional location and ETA

**ETA Monitoring System:**
- GPS tracking shows professional movement toward facility
- Notifications sent to professionals expected to be 30+ minutes late
- Prompts professional to confirm intended arrival
- Facility receives real-time ETA updates

### Billing for Cancellations
**Invoice Reflection:**
- Cancellations within 24 hours of shift start are charged per cancellation rules
- Late cancellations appear on invoices for the pay period
- Detailed line-item breakdown of cancellation fees
- Automatic calculation based on contract terms

## Digital Timesheets and Clock Management

### Digital Timesheet System
**How Digital Timesheets Work:**
- In-app logging and verification of shift hours through Clipboard Health mobile app
- Replaces traditional paper timesheets for most facilities
- Real-time generation of timecard information
- Seamless integration with instant payment system

### Clock In Process
**Requirements for Successful Clock In:**
- Device must be connected to the internet
- Location services must be enabled
- Professional must be physically inside the facility building
- GPS geofencing ensures location accuracy

**NFC Time Clock Integration:**
- Many facilities require NFC time clock poster interaction
- Professionals must tap mobile device against NFC poster
- Additional security layer confirming physical presence
- Required for both clock in and clock out processes

### Clock Out and Approval Process
**Clock Out Procedure:**
- Professional clocks out through mobile app at shift end
- Digital timesheet automatically generated with timecard information
- Facility staff signature required for timesheet submission
- GPS and NFC verification for location confirmation

**Facility Approval Process:**
- Facility staff can adjust times during digital signature process
- Clock out time can be edited by facility supervisor
- Adjustments limited to 2 hours beyond scheduled shift end time
- Extended shifts beyond 2 hours require customer support contact

**Time Editing Restrictions:**
- Some facilities disable in-app timesheet editing
- Professionals cannot edit times at facilities with restrictions
- All time adjustments must be made by facility staff
- Ensures facility control over timesheet accuracy

### Break Time Management
**Automatic Breaks:**
- System can automatically calculate required break times
- Worked break payment requests can be submitted
- Compliance with labor law break requirements
- Facility-specific break policies configurable

### Paper Timesheet Alternative
**When Paper Timesheets Are Used:**
- Facilities without digital timesheet implementation
- Backup option for technical issues
- Professional must upload paper timesheet photo
- Less preferred method due to processing delays

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Platform Questions

**Q: What is Clipboard Health?**
A: Clipboard Health is a comprehensive healthcare staffing platform connecting healthcare facilities with licensed professionals for shift-based work through mobile and web applications.

**Q: How does Clipboard Health make money?**
A: Clipboard Health charges facilities hourly rates for shifts booked through the platform. Professionals receive instant payment after completing shifts and submitting timesheets.

**Q: Who can work through Clipboard Health?**
A: Licensed healthcare professionals including CNAs, LVNs, RNs, and other healthcare specialties can work through the platform after completing document verification and onboarding.

### Shift Management Questions

**Q: When should facilities post shifts?**
A: Shifts should be posted as soon as they become available rather than waiting until closer to the scheduled date, as earlier posting significantly increases booking likelihood.

**Q: What happens if a professional cancels last minute?**
A: Cancellations within 0-8 hours before or 0-2 hours after shift start automatically trigger Urgent Shifts at premium rates to attract replacement coverage at no additional cost to facilities.

**Q: Can facilities invite specific professionals to work shifts?**
A: Yes, the Shift Invites feature allows facilities to invite specific professionals to work particular shifts with custom rates and exclusive access periods.

### Billing and Payment Questions

**Q: How are facilities billed for shifts?**
A: Facilities are charged hourly rates for booked shifts, with invoices reflecting filled shifts and any applicable cancellation fees based on contract terms.

**Q: What payment methods are available?**
A: ACH payment is recommended for faster processing and reduced fees. Payments are processed through Invoiced.com integration with autopay options available.

**Q: What is Extra Time Pay?**
A: Extra Time Pay is an automatic incentive for professionals working 40+ hours per calendar week or consecutive hour thresholds, with adjustable multipliers set by facilities.

### Document and Onboarding Questions

**Q: What documents are required to work as a professional?**
A: Required documents include government-issued photo ID with selfie verification, background check through Checkr, professional license, and Social Security Number for compliance verification.

**Q: How long does document verification take?**
A: Document verification is conducted by the Onboarding and Compliance team with thorough review processes. Timeline varies based on document complexity and completeness.

**Q: Can facilities modify document requirements?**
A: Yes, facilities can request modifications to document requirements by contacting onboarding@clipboardhealth.com or submitting a Facility Requirement Update Request Form.

### Technical Support Questions

**Q: What should I do if I can't log into my account?**
A: Ensure you have the latest app version, try closing and relaunching the app, visit https://hcp.clipboard.health for new verification links, and check spam folders for verification emails.

**Q: How do I contact support?**
A: Support is available 24/7 at (408) 837-0116, through the AI Virtual Assistant in the app (My Account > Help Center > Start a Chat), or by submitting requests at support.clipboardhealth.com.

**Q: What browsers work best with the web portal?**
A: Google Chrome is recommended for optimal compatibility. Clear cache and cookies regularly and ensure JavaScript is enabled for full functionality.

### Digital Timesheet Questions

**Q: How do digital timesheets work?**
A: Professionals clock in/out through the mobile app using GPS geofencing and often NFC time clock posters. Facility staff must digitally sign timesheets for submission and payment processing.

**Q: Can facility staff adjust timesheet times?**
A: Yes, facility staff can adjust times during the digital signature process, with clock out times adjustable up to 2 hours beyond scheduled shift end. Extended adjustments require customer support assistance.

**Q: What if my facility doesn't use digital timesheets?**
A: Some facilities still use paper timesheets, which professionals must photograph and upload through the app. Digital timesheets are preferred for faster processing and instant payment eligibility.
