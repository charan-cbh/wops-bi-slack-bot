# Clipboard Health Business Context

## Platform Overview

Clipboard Health is a comprehensive healthcare staffing platform that connects facilities with healthcare professionals (CNAs, LVNs, RNs, and more) for shift-based work. The platform operates through both mobile applications and a web portal, providing real-time scheduling, billing, and workforce management solutions.

### Core Business Model
- Facilities post shifts on the platform marketplace
- Healthcare professionals book available shifts
- Clipboard Health charges facilities hourly rates for booked shifts
- All shifts are eligible for instant payment to workers

## Shift Management & Booking System

### Shift Posting Process
- **Early Posting Strategy**: Shifts should be posted as soon as available rather than waiting closer to scheduled dates, as earlier posting increases booking likelihood
- **Multi-Platform Access**: Shifts can be posted from mobile phones using the Clipboard Facility app or from desktop through the web portal
- **InstantBook Feature**: The vast majority of facilities have InstantBook enabled, meaning shifts are confirmed immediately when workers book them

### Priority Access System
- **Favorites List**: When shifts are posted, a Priority Access window opens giving professionals on the facility's Favorites List first opportunity to book before general marketplace
- **Shift Invites**: Facilities can invite specific professionals to work shifts, making the shift available only to the invited professional initially

### Urgent Shifts Feature
- **Automatic Triggering**: Urgent Shifts are automatically posted at premium rates when:
  - Professionals cancel within 0-8 hours before or 0-2 hours after shift start time
  - Shifts are removed from professional calendars on short notice
  - No-shows are anticipated
- **Cost Structure**: No additional cost to facilities; premium rates attract coverage for urgent needs

### Skills Assessment Requirements
- **CNA Requirements**: Video-based evaluation for CNAs testing key skills
- **New Professional Requirement**: Required for newer professionals to book shifts at facilities
- **Skills Verification**: Ensures worker competency before shift assignment

## Billing & Payment Structure

### Facility Billing Model
- **Shift Charge Rate**: Hourly base rate that facilities pay for shifts booked through the marketplace
- **Rate Flexibility**: Facilities can adjust charge rates for shifts to align with market demands
- **Competitive Pricing**: Platform recommends setting competitive rates to ensure shifts attract workers

### Payment Processing
- **ACH Payments**: Recommended for faster processing times and reduced transaction fees
- **Invoice Management**: Admin and Invoice role users can view, download, and pay invoices through web portal
- **Third-Party Processing**: Payments processed through Invoiced.com
- **Autopay Option**: Available to ensure timely invoice payments

### Additional Cost Factors
- **Extra Time Pay**: Incentive that facilities can offer for consecutive hours or weekly thresholds (>40 hours/week)
- **Cancellation Fees**: Charges apply for cancellations within 24 hours of scheduled start time
- **Early Departure Fees**: Charges based on when workers are sent home early

### Rate Adjustments
- **Dynamic Pricing**: Facilities can modify charge rates to better align with market demands
- **Invoice Transparency**: Rate adjustments are reflected with line-item details showing who made changes and when
- **Custom Rates**: Available for Shift Invites with facility-specific pricing

## Professional Account Management

### Account Structure
- **My Account Section**: Central hub for professionals to manage accounts, view documents, payment preferences, and update profiles
- **Document Management**: Automated notifications for expiring documents to avoid booking interference
- **Advanced Verification**: Thorough document authentication by Onboarding and Compliance team

### User Access & Permissions
- **Unlimited Users**: Facilities can add unlimited users at no additional cost
- **Role-Based Access**: Different permission levels (Admin, Invoice, standard users)
- **Immediate Access Control**: User access can be removed instantly when no longer working at facility

### Professional Categories
- **Healthcare Professionals**: CNAs, LVNs, RNs, and other healthcare specialties
- **Document Requirements**: State licenses, COVID test status, driver's licenses
- **Booking Eligibility**: Document compliance required for shift booking

### Digital Workflow Management
- **Digital Timesheets**: In-app logging and verification of shift hours
- **Instant Payment**: Workers can receive immediate payment after uploading timesheets
- **Shift History**: Complete record management for professionals and facilities

## Mobile & Desktop Applications

### Clipboard Workplace Mobile App
**Core Features:**
- **Shifts Page**: Post new shifts and manage schedules through calendar view
- **Live ETA**: Track professional's estimated time of arrival
- **Workers Page**: List view of upcoming, in-progress, and past workers
- **Communication**: Messaging, rating, and worker blocking capabilities
- **Facility Management**: View facility info, settings, charges, and invoice history

### Desktop Web Portal
**Key Functionalities:**
- **Comprehensive Shift Management**: Post, delete, or cancel shifts
- **Workplace Rules Management**: Create text fields and quiz generation for professionals
- **Time-Clock Settings**: Configure worker clock in/out and maximum hours restrictions
- **Calendar Integration**: Schedule overview and verification systems
- **Invoice Management**: View, download, and pay invoices

### Platform Integration
- **Cross-Platform Sync**: Mobile and desktop platforms share real-time data
- **User Authentication**: Secure login processes for both interfaces
- **Feature Parity**: Core functionalities available across all platforms

## Key Business Metrics & Performance Indicators

### Operational Metrics
- **Shift Fill Rate**: Percentage of posted shifts successfully filled
- **Booking Speed**: Time between shift posting and professional booking
- **Cancellation Rate**: Percentage of shifts canceled within various time windows
- **Professional Retention**: Worker return rate to facilities

### Financial Metrics
- **Average Charge Rate**: Facility hourly rates across different roles and locations
- **Invoice Processing Time**: Speed of billing and payment collection
- **Premium Shift Revenue**: Income from urgent and high-demand shifts

### Quality Metrics
- **Professional Ratings**: Facility satisfaction scores for workers
- **Document Compliance**: Percentage of professionals with current, verified credentials
- **Platform Uptime**: Technical reliability of mobile and web applications
