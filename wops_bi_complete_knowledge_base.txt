WOPS BI COMPLETE KNOWLEDGE BASE FOR PERFECT SQL QUERY GENERATION

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This knowledge base contains comprehensive business intelligence patterns and query templates for Worker Operations (WOPS) organizational-level KPIs and top-line metrics. The system is designed to generate accurate SQL queries for Metabase dashboards tracking overall ticket volume, organizational response times, and departmental performance metrics across Chat, Voice, and Web channels.

PRIMARY BUSINESS OBJECTIVES - ORGANIZATIONAL LEVEL
1. Monitor overall daily ticket volume across all contact channels for the entire Worker Operations organization
2. Track organizational performance through departmental Average Handle Time (AHT) metrics
3. Analyze overall response time distributions for organizational service quality standards
4. Provide top-line KPIs for leadership reporting and organizational performance tracking
5. Support strategic capacity planning and operational decisions at the organizational level

TICKET VOLUME ANALYTICS

BUSINESS PURPOSE:
Track organizational-level daily ticket volume trends across different contact channels to support strategic capacity planning, departmental performance tracking, and top-line operational KPIs for the entire Worker Operations organization.

USED BY:
- Senior Leadership: Organizational performance dashboards and executive reporting
- Operations Directors: Departmental volume monitoring and strategic planning
- Analytics Team: Top-line KPI reporting and organizational business insights
- Executive Team: High-level operational metrics and organizational performance tracking

PRIMARY TABLE: DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_ZENDESK__MQR_TICKETS

CRITICAL COLUMNS:
- TICKET_ID: Unique identifier for each support ticket
- CREATED_AT_PST: Ticket creation timestamp in Pacific Standard Time
- GROUP_ID: Determines contact channel (17837476387479=Chat, 28949203098007=Voice, 5495272772503=Web)
- STATUS: Ticket resolution status (must be 'closed' or 'solved')
- ASSIGNEE_NAME: Agent assigned to handle the ticket
- TICKET_TAGS: Comma-separated tags applied to tickets
- CHANNEL: Communication method (api, email, native_messaging, web)
- BRAND_ID: Brand identifier for multi-brand filtering
- REPLY_TIME_IN_MINUTES: Time taken for first agent response

CONTACT CHANNEL MAPPING:
The GROUP_ID field maps to contact channels as follows:
- Chat: GROUP_ID = '17837476387479'
- Voice: GROUP_ID = '28949203098007'  
- Web: GROUP_ID = '5495272772503'
- Other: All other GROUP_ID values

MANDATORY FILTERS FOR ALL TICKET QUERIES:
1. Status Filter: STATUS IN ('closed', 'solved') - Only completed tickets
2. Exclude Automated Agents: ASSIGNEE_NAME NOT IN ('Automated Update', 'TechOps Bot') OR ASSIGNEE_NAME IS NULL
3. Exclude System Tags: NOT LOWER(TICKET_TAGS) LIKE '%email_blocked%' AND NOT LOWER(TICKET_TAGS) LIKE '%bulk_email_tool%' AND NOT LOWER(TICKET_TAGS) LIKE '%seattle_psst_monthly_email%'
4. Valid Channels: CHANNEL IN ('api', 'email', 'native_messaging', 'web')
5. Brand Filter: BRAND_ID IN ('29186504989207', '360002340693')

TICKET VOLUME QUERY PATTERNS:

PATTERN 1: Daily Ticket Volume by Single Channel
BUSINESS QUESTIONS: "What's our overall chat volume today?", "Show me organizational voice ticket volume for last week", "Overall daily ticket counts for the department"
SQL TEMPLATE:
SELECT 
    TO_DATE(TO_TIMESTAMP_LTZ(CREATED_AT_PST)) AS date,
    COUNT(DISTINCT TICKET_ID) AS ticket_count
FROM DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_ZENDESK__MQR_TICKETS
WHERE STATUS IN ('closed', 'solved')
    AND (ASSIGNEE_NAME != 'Automated Update' OR ASSIGNEE_NAME IS NULL)
    AND (ASSIGNEE_NAME != 'TechOps Bot' OR ASSIGNEE_NAME IS NULL)
    AND (NOT LOWER(TICKET_TAGS) LIKE '%email_blocked%' OR TICKET_TAGS IS NULL)
    AND (NOT LOWER(TICKET_TAGS) LIKE '%bulk_email_tool%' OR TICKET_TAGS IS NULL)
    AND (NOT LOWER(TICKET_TAGS) LIKE '%seattle_psst_monthly_email%' OR TICKET_TAGS IS NULL)
    AND CHANNEL IN ('api', 'email', 'native_messaging', 'web')
    AND BRAND_ID IN ('29186504989207', '360002340693')
    AND CASE WHEN GROUP_ID = '17837476387479' THEN 'Chat'
             WHEN GROUP_ID = '28949203098007' THEN 'Voice'
             WHEN GROUP_ID = '5495272772503' THEN 'Web'
             ELSE 'Other' END = '{CHANNEL_NAME}'
    AND CREATED_AT_PST >= {START_DATE}
    AND CREATED_AT_PST < {END_DATE}
GROUP BY TO_DATE(TO_TIMESTAMP_LTZ(CREATED_AT_PST))
ORDER BY date ASC

PATTERN 2: Multi-Channel Volume Comparison
BUSINESS QUESTIONS: "Compare all channels", "Which channel has most volume?", "Show ticket volume by channel"
SQL TEMPLATE:
SELECT 
    TO_DATE(TO_TIMESTAMP_LTZ(CREATED_AT_PST)) AS date,
    CASE WHEN GROUP_ID = '17837476387479' THEN 'Chat'
         WHEN GROUP_ID = '28949203098007' THEN 'Voice'
         WHEN GROUP_ID = '5495272772503' THEN 'Web'
         ELSE 'Other' END AS channel,
    COUNT(DISTINCT TICKET_ID) AS ticket_count
FROM DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_ZENDESK__MQR_TICKETS
WHERE STATUS IN ('closed', 'solved')
    AND [STANDARD_FILTERS_APPLIED]
    AND CREATED_AT_PST >= {DATE_RANGE}
GROUP BY date, channel
ORDER BY date ASC, channel

RESPONSE TIME ANALYSIS

BUSINESS PURPOSE:
Analyze how quickly agents respond to customer inquiries to maintain service quality standards and identify areas for improvement.

RESPONSE TIME BUCKETS:
- Excellent: Under 1 minute (REPLY_TIME_IN_MINUTES <= 1)
- Good: 1-15 minutes (REPLY_TIME_IN_MINUTES > 1 AND REPLY_TIME_IN_MINUTES <= 15)
- Acceptable: 15-60 minutes (REPLY_TIME_IN_MINUTES > 15 AND REPLY_TIME_IN_MINUTES <= 60)
- Needs Improvement: Over 1 hour (REPLY_TIME_IN_MINUTES > 60)

RESPONSE TIME QUERY PATTERN:
BUSINESS QUESTIONS: "What's the response time breakdown for chat?", "How quickly do we respond?", "Response time distribution"
SQL TEMPLATE:
SELECT 
    TO_DATE(TO_TIMESTAMP_LTZ(CREATED_AT_PST)) AS date,
    SUM(CASE WHEN REPLY_TIME_IN_MINUTES <= 1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS under_1_min,
    SUM(CASE WHEN REPLY_TIME_IN_MINUTES > 1 AND REPLY_TIME_IN_MINUTES <= 15 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS min_1_to_15,
    SUM(CASE WHEN REPLY_TIME_IN_MINUTES > 15 AND REPLY_TIME_IN_MINUTES <= 60 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS min_15_to_60,
    SUM(CASE WHEN REPLY_TIME_IN_MINUTES > 60 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS over_1_hour
FROM DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_ZENDESK__MQR_TICKETS
WHERE [STANDARD_FILTERS] AND [CHANNEL_FILTER] AND [DATE_FILTER]
GROUP BY TO_DATE(TO_TIMESTAMP_LTZ(CREATED_AT_PST))
ORDER BY date ASC

AHT QUERY PATTERNS:

PATTERN 1: Weekly AHT Trends by Channel
BUSINESS QUESTIONS: "What's our overall organizational chat AHT this week?", "Show me departmental weekly AHT trends", "Overall AHT week over week for the organization"
SQL TEMPLATE:
SELECT 
    DATE_TRUNC('week', CREATED_AT_PST) AS week_start,
    AVG(HANDLE_TIME_IN_MINUTES) AS mean_aht,
    PERCENTILE_CONT(0.5) WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY HANDLE_TIME_IN_MINUTES) AS median_aht,
    COUNT(*) AS ticket_count
FROM DBT_PRODUCTION.ZENDESK_TICKET_AGENT__HANDLE_TIME
WHERE AHT_TYPE = '{AHT_CHANNEL_TYPE}'
    AND HANDLE_TIME_IN_MINUTES >= 2
    AND LOWER(GROUP_NAME) LIKE '%wops%'
    AND CREATED_AT_PST >= {START_DATE}
    AND CREATED_AT_PST < {END_DATE}
GROUP BY DATE_TRUNC('week', CREATED_AT_PST)
ORDER BY week_start ASC

PATTERN 2: Organizational Daily AHT Analysis
BUSINESS QUESTIONS: "What's our daily organizational AHT?", "Show me departmental AHT performance", "Daily AHT trends for the organization"

SELECT

PATTERN 3: Multi-Channel AHT Comparison

ADVANCED QUERY PATTERNS

WEEK OVER WEEK ANALYSIS:
SELECT 
    week_start,
    mean_aht,
    LAG(mean_aht) OVER (ORDER BY week_start) AS prev_week_aht,
    mean_aht - LAG(mean_aht) OVER (ORDER BY week_start) AS wow_change,
    ROUND(((mean_aht - LAG(mean_aht) OVER (ORDER BY week_start)) / LAG(mean_aht) OVER (ORDER BY week_start)) * 100, 2) AS wow_pct_change
FROM (
    SELECT DATE_TRUNC('week', CREATED_AT_PST) AS week_start,
           AVG(HANDLE_TIME_IN_MINUTES) AS mean_aht
    FROM DBT_PRODUCTION.ZENDESK_TICKET_AGENT__HANDLE_TIME
    WHERE [FILTERS]
    GROUP BY week_start
) ORDER BY week_start

AHT DISTRIBUTION ANALYSIS:
SELECT 
    CASE WHEN HANDLE_TIME_IN_MINUTES <= 5 THEN '0-5 min'
         WHEN HANDLE_TIME_IN_MINUTES <= 10 THEN '5-10 min'
         WHEN HANDLE_TIME_IN_MINUTES <= 15 THEN '10-15 min'
         WHEN HANDLE_TIME_IN_MINUTES <= 30 THEN '15-30 min'
         ELSE '30+ min' END AS aht_bucket,
    COUNT(*) AS ticket_count,
    ROUND(COUNT(*) * 100.0 / SUM(COUNT(*)) OVER (), 2) AS percentage
FROM DBT_PRODUCTION.ZENDESK_TICKET_AGENT__HANDLE_TIME
WHERE [FILTERS]
GROUP BY aht_bucket
ORDER BY MIN(HANDLE_TIME_IN_MINUTES)

=== QA SCORE ANALYTICS ===

PRIMARY TABLES:
1. DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_KLAUS__REVIEWS - Klaus QA review data
2. DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_ZENDESK__MQR_TICKETS - Ticket data for joining

KEY QA METRICS:
- Overall_Score: Calculated QA score (0-100 scale)
- Total_Audits: Number of QA reviews completed
- Average_QA_Score: Mean quality score across all audits

SCORECARD FILTERING:
Valid Scorecard IDs: 53921, 54262, 59144, 60047, 56287, 60363
Primary Scorecards for reporting: 53921, 60047

QA SCORE THRESHOLDS:
- Scorecards 53921, 54262, 60047: Pass >= 85%
- Scorecard 59144: Pass >= 90%
- Target Score: 85%+ for most scorecards

QA SCORE QUERY PATTERN:
BUSINESS QUESTIONS: "What's our weekly QA score?", "Show me QA trends", "How many audits this week?"

SQL TEMPLATE:
SELECT 
    DATE_TRUNC('week', tickets.SOLVED_AT_PST) AS week_start,
    AVG(qa_data.Overall_Score) AS average_qa_score,
    COUNT(*) AS total_audits
FROM (
    -- Klaus QA Data Processing CTE
    WITH KlausBase AS (
        SELECT
            klaus.REVIEW_ID,
            REGEXP_REPLACE(TICKET_URL, '.*/tickets/', '') as TICKET_ID,
            SCORECARD_ID,
            SCORECARD_NAME,
            RATING_CATEGORY_ID,
            RATING_SCORE,
            RATING_WEIGHT,
            REVIEWEE_EMAIL,
            REVIEWEE_NAME,
            REVIEW_CREATED_AT
        FROM DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_KLAUS__REVIEWS as klaus
        WHERE SCORECARD_ID IN (53921, 54262, 59144, 60047, 56287, 60363)
    ),
    PivotedRatings AS (
        SELECT
            REVIEW_ID,
            CASE WHEN SUM(RATING_WEIGHT) = 0 THEN NULL
                 ELSE SUM(RATING_SCORE * RATING_WEIGHT) / SUM(100 * RATING_WEIGHT) * 100
            END as Overall_Score
        FROM KlausBase
        GROUP BY REVIEW_ID
    )
    SELECT 
        kb.TICKET_ID,
        kb.SCORECARD_ID,
        pr.Overall_Score
    FROM KlausBase kb
    JOIN PivotedRatings pr ON kb.REVIEW_ID = pr.REVIEW_ID
    WHERE kb.REVIEWEE_EMAIL IS NOT NULL
) qa_data
LEFT JOIN DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_ZENDESK__MQR_TICKETS tickets 
    ON qa_data.TICKET_ID = tickets.TICKET_ID
WHERE qa_data.SCORECARD_ID IN (53921, 60047)
    AND tickets.SOLVED_AT_PST >= {START_DATE}
    AND tickets.SOLVED_AT_PST < {END_DATE}
    AND tickets.STATUS IN ('closed', 'solved')
GROUP BY DATE_TRUNC('week', tickets.SOLVED_AT_PST)
ORDER BY week_start ASC

=== FIRST CONTACT RESOLUTION (FCR) ANALYTICS ===

PRIMARY TABLE: DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_ZENDESK__MQR_TICKETS (with complex FCR logic)

KEY FCR METRICS:
- FCR Rate: Percentage of issues resolved on first contact
- Repeat Contact Volume: Number of follow-up contacts within 24 hours
- IS_RESOLVED_FIRST_TIME: Binary indicator (1 = resolved first time, 0 = repeat contact)
- Contact Channels: Chat and Voice combined for FCR calculation

FCR CALCULATION LOGIC:
A ticket is considered "resolved first time" if:
1. No follow-up ticket from same requester within 24 hours
2. Only applies to Chat and Voice channels
3. Must be from same requester (REQUESTER_ID)

FCR PERFORMANCE BENCHMARKS:
- Excellent: 90%+ FCR rate
- Good: 85%+ FCR rate
- Target: 80%+ FCR rate  
- Needs Improvement: <80% FCR rate

FCR QUERY PATTERN:
BUSINESS QUESTIONS: "What's our FCR rate?", "Show me FCR trends", "Repeat contact volume"

SQL TEMPLATE:
SELECT 
    DATE_TRUNC('week', CREATED_AT_PST) AS week_start,
    (CAST(SUM(CASE WHEN IS_RESOLVED_FIRST_TIME = 1 
                    AND CONTACT_CHANNEL IN ('Chat', 'Voice') THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS DOUBLE) /
     NULLIF(CAST(SUM(CASE WHEN CONTACT_CHANNEL IN ('Chat', 'Voice') THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS DOUBLE), 0)) * 100 AS fcr_rate,
    SUM(CASE WHEN IS_RESOLVED_FIRST_TIME = 0 
             AND CONTACT_CHANNEL IN ('Chat', 'Voice') THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS repeat_contact_volume
FROM (
    WITH FCR_CTE AS (
        SELECT
            ticket_id,
            requester_id,
            CASE WHEN group_id = '17837476387479' THEN 'Chat'
                 WHEN group_id = '28949203098007' THEN 'Voice'
                 ELSE 'Other' END AS contact_channel,
            created_at_pst,
            CASE WHEN created_at_pst + INTERVAL '24 HOUR' >= 
                      LEAD(created_at_pst) OVER (PARTITION BY requester_id ORDER BY created_at_pst)
                 THEN 0 ELSE 1 END AS is_resolved_first_time
        FROM DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_ZENDESK__MQR_TICKETS
        WHERE STATUS IN ('solved', 'closed')
            AND brand_id IN ('360002340693', '29186504989207')
            AND channel IN ('native_messaging')
            AND group_id IN ('17837476387479', '28949203098007')
            AND ASSIGNEE_ID != '15840273336343'
            AND TICKET_TAGS NOT LIKE '%email_blocked%'
            AND TICKET_TAGS NOT LIKE '%bulk_email_tool%'
            AND TICKET_TAGS NOT LIKE '%seattle_psst_monthly_email%'
            AND created_at_pst >= {DATE_RANGE}
    )
    SELECT * FROM FCR_CTE
) fcr_data
WHERE CREATED_AT_PST >= {START_DATE}
    AND CREATED_AT_PST < {END_DATE}
GROUP BY DATE_TRUNC('week', CREATED_AT_PST)
ORDER BY week_start ASC
