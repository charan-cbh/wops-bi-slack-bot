USED BY:

=== KEY PERFORMANCE METRICS ===

METRIC 1: Average Handle Time (AHT)
Source: DBT_PRODUCTION.ZENDESK_TICKET_AGENT__HANDLE_TIME
Calculation: AVG(HANDLE_TIME_IN_MINUTES) for Voice + Chat combined
Filter: AHT_TYPE IN ('AHT - Voice', 'AHT - Chat')
Group By: SUPERVISOR, Week

METRIC 2: Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)  
Source: DBT_PRODUCTION.RPT_CSAT_SURVEY_DETAILS
Calculation: AVG(CSAT_SCORE_BINARY)
Group By: ASSIGNEE_SUPERVISOR, Week

METRIC 3: Schedule Adherence
Source: DBT_PRODUCTION.RPT_AGENT_SCHEDULE_ADHERENCE
Calculation: SUM(ADHERENT_MINUTES) / SUM(SCHEDULED_MINUTES)
Join: Via AGENT_EMAIL to get SUPERVISOR
Group By: SUPERVISOR, Week

METRIC 4: QA Score
Source: DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_KLAUS__REVIEWS + Complex Klaus CTE
Calculation: AVG(Overall_Score) from Klaus scorecard processing
Filter: SCORECARD_ID = '53921' (primary scorecard)
Join: Via REVIEWEE_EMAIL to DIM_ZENDESK_USERS.SUPERVISOR
Group By: SUPERVISOR, Week

METRIC 5: First Contact Resolution (FCR)
Source: DBT_PRODUCTION.FCT_ZENDESK__MQR_TICKETS with FCR logic
Calculation: (Resolved First Time / Total Contacts) * 100
Channels: Chat and Voice only
Group By: ASSIGNEE_SUPERVISOR, Week

=== TEAM LEAD PERFORMANCE QUERY PATTERN ===

BUSINESS QUESTIONS: "How is [TL_NAME] performing?", "Team lead performance this week", "Show me supervisor metrics"

SQL TEMPLATE STRUCTURE:
SELECT 
    DATE_TRUNC('week', SOLVED_AT_PST) AS week_start,
    "Dim Zendesk Users - Assignee Supervisor"."SUPERVISOR" AS supervisor_name,
    AVG(aht_metrics.AHT) AS avg_aht,
    AVG(csat_metrics.CSAT) AS avg_csat, 
    AVG(adherence_metrics.adherence_pct) AS avg_adherence,
    AVG(qa_metrics.qa_score) AS avg_qa_score,
    AVG(fcr_metrics.fcr_rate) AS avg_fcr
FROM base_ticket_data
LEFT JOIN aht_weekly_data ON (week match + supervisor match)
LEFT JOIN csat_weekly_data ON (week match + supervisor match)  
LEFT JOIN adherence_weekly_data ON (week match + supervisor match)
LEFT JOIN qa_weekly_data ON (week match + supervisor match)
LEFT JOIN fcr_weekly_data ON (week match + supervisor match)
WHERE supervisor filtering for active TLs
    AND date_range_filtering
GROUP BY week_start, supervisor_name
ORDER BY week_start ASC, supervisor_name ASC

=== INDIVIDUAL METRIC QUERY PATTERNS ===

AHT BY TEAM LEAD:
BUSINESS QUESTIONS: "Show me Joan Mallari's AHT performance", "Team lead AHT trends"
SELECT 
    DATE_TRUNC('week', SOLVED_AT_PST) AS week_start,
    SUPERVISOR,
    AVG(CASE WHEN AHT_TYPE IN ('AHT - Voice', 'AHT - Chat') 
             THEN HANDLE_TIME_IN_MINUTES END) AS avg_aht_minutes
FROM DBT_PRODUCTION.ZENDESK_TICKET_AGENT__HANDLE_TIME
WHERE HANDLE_TIME_IN_MINUTES >= 2
    AND date_range_filtering
    AND supervisor_filtering
GROUP BY week_start, SUPERVISOR

CSAT BY TEAM LEAD:
BUSINESS QUESTIONS: "Team lead CSAT performance", "Which supervisors have low CSAT?"
SELECT 
    DATE_TRUNC('week', SOLVED_AT_PST) AS week_start,
    ASSIGNEE_SUPERVISOR,
    AVG(CSAT_SCORE_BINARY) AS avg_csat_score,
    COUNT(*) AS total_surveys
FROM DBT_PRODUCTION.RPT_CSAT_SURVEY_DETAILS  
WHERE date_range_filtering
GROUP BY week_start, ASSIGNEE_SUPERVISOR

QA SCORE BY TEAM LEAD:
BUSINESS QUESTIONS: "Team lead quality performance", "QA coaching needs by supervisor"
Uses complex Klaus CTE processing + supervisor join via REVIEWEE_EMAIL
Filter: SCORECARD_ID = '53921' for primary QA scorecard
Calculation: AVG(Overall_Score) grouped by supervisor and week

FCR BY TEAM LEAD:
BUSINESS QUESTIONS: "Team lead resolution efficiency", "First contact performance by supervisor"
Uses complex FCR CTE with 24-hour repeat contact logic
Channels: Chat and Voice only (group_id filtering)
Calculation: (First time resolved / Total contacts) * 100

ADHERENCE BY TEAM LEAD:
BUSINESS QUESTIONS: "Schedule adherence by team lead", "Which supervisors need attendance coaching?"
Calculation: SUM(ADHERENT_MINUTES) / SUM(SCHEDULED_MINUTES)
Requires join from AGENT_EMAIL to DIM_ZENDESK_USERS.SUPERVISOR

=== PERFORMANCE BENCHMARKS ===

TEAM LEAD TARGETS:
- AHT (Chat + Voice): <15 minutes average across team
- CSAT: >85% satisfaction rate  
- Schedule Adherence: >90% adherence rate
- QA Score: >85% average quality score
- FCR: >80% first contact resolution rate

COACHING THRESHOLDS:
- Needs Attention: Any metric below target for 2+ consecutive weeks
- Critical: Any metric below 80% of target
- Excellent: All metrics above target consistently
