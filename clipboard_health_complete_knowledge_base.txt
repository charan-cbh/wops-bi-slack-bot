EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Clipboard Health is a comprehensive healthcare staffing platform connecting healthcare facilities with licensed professionals (CNAs, LVNs, RNs, and other specialties) for shift-based work. Founded by Wei Deng with the mission to "move as many people up the socioeconomic ladder as possible," the company is a fast-growing Series C marketplace, profitable since 2022, filling millions of shifts annually across the US.

The platform operates 24/7 through mobile applications and web portals, providing real-time scheduling, billing, workforce management, instant payment processing, and comprehensive compliance management for the healthcare industry.

CORE PLATFORM OVERVIEW

What is Clipboard Health?
Clipboard Health is a technology-enabled healthcare staffing marketplace that dramatically improves lives by letting healthcare professionals turn extra time and ambition into career growth and financial opportunity. The robust tech platform makes it easy for healthcare professionals and healthcare facilities to form relationships needed to care for patients and address talent shortages.

Business Model Structure:
- Facilities post available shifts on the platform marketplace
- Healthcare professionals browse and book available shifts using mobile app or web portal
- Clipboard Health charges facilities hourly rates for booked shifts based on contract terms
- All shifts are eligible for instant payment to workers after timesheet submission
- Platform operates with automated systems and human support available 24/7
- Independent contractor model - professionals are not employees but contractors

Key Value Propositions:
For Facilities: Instant access to qualified healthcare professionals, reduced administrative burden, flexible staffing solutions, compliance management, real-time communication tools
For Professionals: Flexible work schedules, instant payment options, access to multiple facilities, professional development opportunities, career growth potential

COMPREHENSIVE SHIFT MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Shift Posting Process and Best Practices:
- Post shifts as soon as they become available rather than waiting closer to scheduled dates
- Earlier posting significantly increases booking likelihood by up to 300%
- Shifts can be posted from mobile app, desktop web portal, or bulk posting tools
- Use competitive rates based on market analysis to ensure shifts attract qualified professionals
- Consider geographic, specialty, and time-of-day factors when setting rates

Priority Access System:
- Favorites List: Professionals on facility's favorites list receive priority booking window
- Priority Window: Opens when shifts are first posted, lasting up to 6 hours before general marketplace access
- A-Team System: Top-ranking professionals get enhanced priority access to all shifts
- InstantBook Feature: Majority of facilities have this enabled for immediate shift confirmation upon booking

Advanced Shift Features:

Shift Invites System:
- Facilities can invite specific professionals to work particular shifts
- Invited shifts are visible only to the invited professional initially with exclusive access period
- Option to open to favorites list if invitation is declined within specified timeframe
- Custom rates can be set for invited professionals, including premium pricing
- Works with both individual shifts and block bookings

Block Booking Functionality:
- Feature allows posting blocks of shifts across 1 or more weeks for single professional booking
- Professionals commit to entire block (80% minimum attendance required)
- Found in "Blocks" tab next to "Per Diem" in Find Shifts section
- Priority Access available for blocks (up to 6 hours for favorites)
- No rate negotiation currently available for blocks
- Future expansion planned to include housing and daily stipends
- Geographic restrictions apply - must have license to practice in area
- Mutual commitment expected between facility and professional for all shifts in block

Urgent Shifts System:

Automatic Triggering Conditions:
- Professional cancels within 0-8 hours before shift start time
- Professional cancels within 0-2 hours after shift start time
- Shifts removed from professional calendars on short notice due to attendance issues
- Anticipated no-shows based on GPS tracking and ETA monitoring
- System automatically detects when professionals aren't moving toward facility

Urgent Shift Benefits and Operations:
- Posted at premium rates (typically 20-50% above standard rates) to attract immediate coverage
- No additional cost to facilities beyond standard contract rates
- Automated system reduces administrative burden on facility staff
- Higher likelihood of last-minute coverage (>90% fill rate for urgent shifts)

Magic Shifts Feature:

- Only applies to CNA/LVN/LPN/RN postings posted at least 1 hour in advance
- Limited number of free Magic Shifts provided monthly to facilities

Manual Urgent Shift Requests:

COMPREHENSIVE FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

General Platform Questions and Detailed Answers:

Q: What is Clipboard Health and how does it work?
A: Clipboard Health is a comprehensive healthcare staffing platform that connects healthcare facilities with licensed professionals (CNAs, LVNs, RNs, and other specialties) for shift-based work. The platform operates through mobile applications and web portals, providing real-time scheduling, billing, workforce management, and instant payment processing. Founded with the mission to move healthcare professionals up the socioeconomic ladder, it serves as a marketplace where facilities post shifts and professionals book available work.

Q: How does Clipboard Health generate revenue?
A: Clipboard Health charges facilities hourly rates for shifts booked through the platform based on individual contract terms. The company operates on a marketplace model where they earn revenue from facility payments while professionals receive their earnings through instant payment processing after completing shifts and submitting digital timesheets.

Q: Who is eligible to work through Clipboard Health?
A: Licensed healthcare professionals including Certified Nursing Assistants (CNAs), Licensed Vocational Nurses (LVNs), Licensed Practical Nurses (LPNs), Registered Nurses (RNs), and other healthcare specialties can work through the platform. All professionals must complete comprehensive document verification and onboarding processes including background checks, license verification, and skills assessments where applicable.

Shift Management Questions and Comprehensive Answers:

Q: When should facilities post shifts for optimal booking success?
A: Shifts should be posted as soon as they become available rather than waiting until closer to the scheduled date. Earlier posting significantly increases booking likelihood by up to 300% because it allows professionals more time to plan their schedules and provides access to the priority access system for favorites and A-team professionals.

Q: What happens when a professional cancels a shift at the last minute?
A: Cancellations within 0-8 hours before or 0-2 hours after shift start automatically trigger the Urgent Shifts system, which reposts the shift at premium rates (typically 20-50% above standard rates) to attract immediate replacement coverage at no additional cost to facilities. The system has over 90% success rate for filling urgent shifts.

Q: How does the Shift Invites feature work for facilities?
A: The Shift Invites feature allows facilities to invite specific professionals to work particular shifts with exclusive access periods. Invited shifts are visible only to the invited professional initially, with options to open to the favorites list if the invitation is declined. Custom rates can be set for invited professionals, and the system works with both individual shifts and block bookings.

Q: What is Block Booking and how does it work?
A: Block Booking allows facilities to post a series of shifts across one or more weeks for a single professional to book in its entirety. Professionals must commit to at least 80% of the block, and mutual commitment is expected for all shifts. Block bookings can include Priority Access for facility favorites and are designed for consistent staffing needs across multiple days or weeks.

Billing and Payment Questions and Detailed Answers:

Q: How are facilities billed for shifts and what costs are involved?
A: Facilities are charged hourly rates for booked shifts based on individual contract terms, with invoices reflecting filled shifts and any applicable cancellation fees. Additional costs may include Extra Time Pay for professionals working over 40 hours per week or extended consecutive shifts, and cancellation fees for shifts canceled within 24 hours of start time based on contract specifications.

Q: What payment methods are available and which is recommended?
A: ACH payment is strongly recommended for faster processing (1-2 business days), reduced transaction fees, and enhanced security. Payments are processed through secure Invoiced.com integration with autopay options available. Credit card payments are accepted but incur higher processing fees and longer processing times.

Q: How does Extra Time Pay work and how is it calculated?
A: Extra Time Pay is an automatic incentive system with two types: Weekly Extra Time Pay for professionals working 40+ hours per calendar week (calculated Sunday to Saturday), and Consecutive Hours Extra Time Pay for extended single shifts. Multipliers are typically 1.5x after 8 hours and 2.0x after 12 hours, with facilities able to customize parameters based on operational needs.

Document and Onboarding Questions and Comprehensive Answers:

Digital Timesheet Questions and Comprehensive Answers:

Q: How do digital timesheets work and what are the requirements?
A: Digital timesheets allow professionals to clock in/out through the mobile app using GPS geofencing and often NFC time clock posters for enhanced security. Requirements include: device connected to internet, location services enabled, physical presence inside facility building, and NFC interaction where required. Facility staff must digitally sign timesheets for final submission and payment processing.

Q: Can facility staff adjust timesheet times and what are the limitations?
A: Yes, facility staff can adjust start and end times during the digital signature process. Clock out times can be adjusted up to 2 hours beyond scheduled shift end time. Adjustments beyond 2 hours require customer support assistance. Some facilities disable in-app timesheet editing, requiring all adjustments to be made by authorized facility staff.

Q: What happens if a facility doesn't use digital timesheets?
A: Some facilities still use paper timesheets as backup or primary method. Professionals must photograph and upload paper timesheets through the mobile app. However, digital timesheets are strongly preferred because they enable faster processing, instant payment eligibility, better accuracy, and reduced administrative overhead for both professionals and facilities.
